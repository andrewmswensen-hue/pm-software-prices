What is the Boom Platform? BoomCRM? BoomScreen? BoomReport? The Boom Platform is where partners can access and manage their Boom products: BoomCRM , BoomScreen , and BoomReport . BoomCRM is an AI-powered leasing & touring CRM that captures, qualifies, and works every lead around the clock. BoomScreen is an intelligent underwriting tool to mitigate fraud and increase leasing velocity. BoomReport is an automated rent reporting solution to help renters build credit with rent. Together they cover the full renter lifecycle, from lead to lease to resident. Does Boom integrate with my property management software? Boom currently integrates with Buildium Premium, Entrata, ManageAmerica, Quext, Propertyware, Rent Manager, Rentvine, ResMan, Yardi, and AppFolio (Lite). If your PMS isn&#x27;t listed, reach out to the Boom team. We&#x27;re always working on adding new integrations. What does Boom&#x27;s Leasing Agent do? Boom&#x27;s Leasing Agent answers every inbound call, 24 hours a day. It answers questions, schedules tours, and sends application links. Every call is logged in BoomCRM with a transcript and a lead record, so your team can see what happened without listening back. Who do my renters and applicants reach out to for customer support? Renters and applicants can reach out to Boom&#x27;s customer support team via the Boom app (BoomReport), via their rental application (BoomScreen), or by emailing hello@boompay.app . The team is online 24/7 to help your renters and applicants with any issues or questions that may arise. One system, from first inquiry to signed lease.
