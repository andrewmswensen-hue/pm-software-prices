Schedule a Demo FAQ What Tenant Turner Is What is Tenant Turner? Tenant Turner is an easy-to-use leasing automation platform that helps property managers get up and running fast. From lead qualification and scheduling to self-showings, follow-up, reporting, and integrated smart access, Tenant Turner automates leasing without adding complexity. How does Tenant Turner work? When a prospect inquires about a rental — by phone, email, or text — Tenant Turner responds automatically with customized messaging and presents available showing times based on your configured availability. The prospect self-screens by answering your qualification questions, selects a time, and receives automated confirmations and reminders. For self-guided tours, Tenant Turner coordinates property access through integrated smart locks or lockboxes. After the showing, it follows up automatically to collect feedback. Your team is involved only when the situation calls for it. Who is Tenant Turner designed for? Tenant Turner is built for residential property managers who want leasing automation that works from day one — without a long setup or ongoing configuration. It&#8217;s a strong fit for teams managing single-family or mixed portfolios who want a clean, consistent workflow and proven best practices built in. Whether you&#8217;re a small team looking to stop scheduling showings manually or a growing operation that needs to scale without adding headcount, Tenant Turner gets you up and running fast. Does Tenant Turner work in Canada? Yes, Tenant Turner works in Canada. Phone and text-based prospect communication features operate in both the U.S. and Canada.
