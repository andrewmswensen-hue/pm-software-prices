How does the 14-day free trial work? Try the full platform for 14 days. A credit card is required, and trial communications and AI usage have limits. Review your selected plan and first charge when you sign up. Your subscription starts when the trial ends unless you cancel beforehand. Do I pay for every teammate? One and Max include unlimited teammates. Sales includes one user and one CRM pipeline, with no per-door fee. Talk with us about additional Sales users and pipelines; your quote will show the full price and allowances for your team. Can I start with just my sales team? Yes. Sales brings CRM, Phone, Inbox, and a monthly AI voice allowance together to help your team win new owners. It includes sales workflows, with no per-door fee. Choose One or Max when you need Operations, Leasing, and Maintenance alongside sales. What can change my monthly bill? One and Max charge for doors above their included allowances; Sales has no per-door fee. Additional CRM pipelines on One, quoted Sales users and pipelines, communications beyond included usage, additional phone numbers, additional services, and applicable taxes can increase your bill. All prices are in US dollars. What does an annual commitment mean? An annual commitment is a 12-month agreement billed monthly. One and Max keep the same monthly minimum, with a lower rate for additional doors; Max also includes more doors. Sales is $129 per month with an annual commitment or $149 month-to-month, with the same included usage on either commitment. How are messaging and AI voice billed? Max includes normal business texting within fair use. AI voice is separate from human calling: Sales includes 25 minutes, One includes 50, and Max includes 500.
