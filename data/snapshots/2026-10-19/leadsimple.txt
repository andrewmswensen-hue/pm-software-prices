A little more clarity Good questions. Simple answers. Still thinking it through? We’re here to help. Let’s talk What is the difference between One and Max? Both include every product, unlimited teammates, and unlimited Operations workflows. Max includes more doors, unlimited CRM pipelines, more AI voice minutes, and unlimited human calling and texting within fair use. Choose the plan that fits your portfolio and how your team works. How does the 14-day free trial work? Try the full platform for 14 days. A credit card is required, and trial communications and AI usage have limits. Review your selected plan and first charge when you sign up. Your subscription starts when the trial ends unless you cancel beforehand. Do I pay for every teammate? No. One and Max both include unlimited teammates, so your whole team can work together in One. How are additional doors priced? One includes 50 doors and Max includes 100. Each door beyond that is billed monthly at the rate of the band it falls in, so doors 251–1,000 get the second rate and doors above 1,000 the lowest. Rates are the same on both plans. Per-door rates with an annual commitment: up to 250 doors, $1.99; 251–1,000 doors, $1.49; over 1,000 doors, $1.29. Month-to-month: up to 250 doors, $2.49; 251–1,000 doors, $1.99; over 1,000 doors, $1.79. What can change my monthly bill? One and Max charge for doors above their included allowances, at a per-door rate that lowers as your portfolio grows. CRM pipelines on One beyond the first 2, communications beyond included usage, additional phone numbers, additional services, and applicable taxes can increase your bill. All prices are in US dollars. What does an annual commitment mean? An annual commitment is a 12-month agreement, still billed monthly. It lowers the monthly price to $169 for One and $429 for Max, compared with $199 and $499 month-to-month, and lowers the rate for every additional door. Included doors and usage allowances are the same on either commitment. How are messaging and AI voice billed? One includes 1,000 messaging credits per month. One SMS segment uses one credit and one MMS uses three. Additional credits are $0.025 each. Max includes normal business texting within fair use. AI voice is separate from human calling: One includes 50 minutes and Max includes 500. Additional AI minutes are $0.40 on both plans. Allowances reset monthly and do not roll over.
